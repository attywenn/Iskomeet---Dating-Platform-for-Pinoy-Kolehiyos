# PRIORITIES WHEN GENERATING CODES AND FILES
    - Do not generate files or codes that are unnecessary or are not part of the prompted request
    - Avoid using gradient that are common for generic AI-made UIs
    - Do not ever use emojis when inserting icons
    - Avoid too much generation or insertion of descriptions or words as this cause redundancy
    - Prioritize simplization of code, do not generate bunch of lines of codes that are completely unnecessary and are lines of codes that only cause redundancy. Focus on the prompt request only, do not decide on your own for what features should be added alongside with the prompted request.
